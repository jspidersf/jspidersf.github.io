San Diego: five hotel bases for November 11–14

Kona Kai is the best overall resort choice; Hotel Indigo is the best value if both full days are for the parks. Omni adds a downtown pool, Seabird favors LEGOLAND and beach time, and Alma is the boutique alternative with more family-room questions.

Check in Wednesday, November 11, 2026; check out Saturday, November 14. That leaves two full days. Spending more on a resort makes sense only if you will use it.

  • 1. Kona Kai San Diego
  • 2. Hotel Indigo San Diego Gaslamp Quarter
  • 3. Omni San Diego Hotel
  • 4. The Seabird Ocean Resort & Spa, Oceanside
  • 5. Alma San Diego Downtown, a Tribute Portfolio Hotel

Thursday, November 12: San Diego Zoo

The Zoo is in Balboa Park, not the Safari Park in Escondido. Posted hours are 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Official Zoo planning and hours.

Friday, November 13: LEGOLAND

LEGOLAND is in Carlsbad. Posted Friday hours are 10 a.m.–5 p.m., longer than Thursday’s 10:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Official LEGOLAND calendar. Recheck both parks before travel.

Car tradeoff: a rental car is strongly useful for this two-park plan. Hotel parking and attraction parking add to the bill; Zoo parking is paid. Downtown dinners may be walkable, but repeated long-distance rideshares to LEGOLAND can reduce that advantage. There is no perfect midpoint among these five choices.

Drive ranges below are rough hotel-to-attraction planning estimates, not live route quotes. I-5 traffic can push trips beyond these ranges. Allow additional time to park, walk to the entrance and manage queues.

1. Kona Kai San Diego

Best overall for a resort stay

Choose this if the hotel is part of the holiday: pool time, marina walks and a quieter Shelter Island setting.

Pros

  • Bayview Two Queen room sleeps four; the quoted room category was checked.
  • Family Tiki pool, marina walks and optional paid bonfires offer things to do between outings.

Cons

  • The sandy beach has no water access: it is not a swimming beach. The second pool is adults-only.
  • Farther south than the other choices, with longer park drives and overnight parking costs.

San Diego Zoo: 20–30 minutes by car.

LEGOLAND: 50–75 minutes by car.

Parking and car: Chase listed parking at $54.88 per night (about $165 for three nights). Compare the direct parking package before choosing a rate.

Chase displayed $1,610; a conditional $250 Edit statement credit would make that $1,360 before extra charges. The matching direct quote was $1,247.59, including taxes and resort fees; a direct parking package was $1,406.95.

After that conditional credit, Edit is still about $112 above the direct room rate. It adds daily breakfast for two and one $100 restricted property credit per stay, subject to terms. The fee wording conflicts ($60/night in Chase property text versus $48 direct) despite an all-in Chase headline. Do not automatically add it twice. Cancellation also differs: Chase November 9 midnight versus a general 72-hour policy and direct November 8 at 3 p.m. Confirm the final checkout terms.

2. Hotel Indigo San Diego Gaslamp Quarter

Best value for two full park days

Choose this when most waking hours will be at the Zoo and LEGOLAND, with downtown restaurants for dinner.

Pros

  • Lowest displayed price in this five-hotel shortlist; standard two-queen room with maximum occupancy four was verified.
  • Short drive to the Zoo and walkable downtown dining.

Cons

  • No pool: choose it for a useful city base rather than resort downtime.
  • Gaslamp/East Village is an urban nightlife setting; parking and the LEGOLAND drive still need budgeting.

San Diego Zoo: 10–20 minutes by car.

LEGOLAND: 45–70 minutes by car.

Parking and car: A rental car is useful for the parks, but confirm the hotel’s current overnight parking charge. Walk to local dinners when practical.

Chase displayed $1,137; a conditional $250 select-hotel statement credit would make that $887 before extra charges. Matching direct quotes were $1,015.65 member / $1,062.14 public.

The direct quote had no deposit and cancellation until November 9 at 6 p.m.; the Chase qualifying Pay Now quote showed November 9 midnight. Confirm portal service fees and the precise cancellation time zone at checkout.

4. The Seabird Ocean Resort & Spa, Oceanside

Best LEGOLAND-first beach splurge

Choose this if Oceanside, the beach and LEGOLAND matter more than a short Zoo drive.

Pros

  • Heated rooftop pool, beach across the street and Oceanside’s pier-town setting.
  • Clearly the easiest LEGOLAND base of these five hotels.

Cons

  • Most expensive displayed quote; the Zoo becomes the long excursion.
  • Exact quoted room, occupancy and final booking terms remain unverified.

San Diego Zoo: 50–75+ minutes by car.

LEGOLAND: 15–25 minutes by car.

Parking and car: Official parking information conflicted at $55 versus $60 per night. Budget about $60 nightly ($180 for three nights) and confirm before booking.

5. Alma San Diego Downtown, a Tribute Portfolio Hotel

Boutique city alternative with limitations

Choose this if boutique style and downtown access matter more than larger beds and simple family logistics.

Pros

  • Boutique downtown setting, rooftop pool and a short drive to the Zoo.
  • A lower displayed price than Kona Kai, Omni or Seabird before parking.

Cons

  • Quoted room has two double beds in about 325 sq ft; explicit maximum occupancy was not shown even though it appeared in a family search.
  • Valet is $70/night. Leave of Absence rooftop restaurant is closed for fall/winter until May 2027; this does not mean the pool is closed.

San Diego Zoo: 10–15 minutes by car.

LEGOLAND: 45–70 minutes by car.

Parking and car: Valet adds $210 over three nights before any additional tax. This can materially erode the apparent room-price saving.

Chase displayed $1,342; a conditional $250 Edit statement credit would make that $1,092 before extra charges.

Chase showed cancellation until November 10 midnight. Confirm occupancy for four, final fees and the time zone. No matching direct quote was verified. Edit breakfast is for two; upgrades and other benefits are subject to terms and availability.
