## Destination judgment

[Astra's independent review](destination-review-2026-10-27.md) separates destination fit from the ease of obtaining quotes. Coastal Benalmádena/Fuengirola remains the first hotel comparison for daily convenience and service. Torre del Mar is a credible apartment alternative; La Vega's separate bedrooms, two bathrooms and pool outweigh Torre Plaza's lower price for a family wanting pool time, subject to actual pool operation and access.

The strongest argument against automatically choosing La Vega is the household work of an apartment, unresolved cancellation terms and less-verified unit/route evidence. Its lower price is meaningful but does not prove greater parental ease. Conversely, hotel rooms sacrifice shared living space and may not be adjacent. The family needs to choose that tradeoff rather than assume either accommodation type wins.

Protur now merits a real all-inclusive comparison: €6,062.40 is only €112.85 above Best and €66.65 above Mac, though meals, taxes and transport are not fully like-for-like. It has two bedrooms plus a sofa bed rather than the apartment three-bedroom layout. Roquetas is in Almería, east of Málaga, and should rise because of resort benefits only if the family's flights/transfers work. Current airfare and transfer quotes were not refreshed.

Central Estepona and central La Cala remain good destination ideas without a new qualifying checkout offer in this pass. Conil is an Atlantic alternative, not the default: more totality does not remove its morning marine-cloud exposure or the tension with no eclipse-day driving. Eclipsophile supports the broad Mediterranean preference, not precise town-by-town success probabilities or a forecast. An unobstructed eastward sightline still needs checking at the actual walking viewpoint; a beach or sea view alone is insufficient. Sources: [Eclipsophile 2027](https://eclipsophile.com/tse2027/) and [IGN 2027 eclipse](https://eclipses.ign.es/eclipse-total-sol-de-2-de-agosto-2027.html).
