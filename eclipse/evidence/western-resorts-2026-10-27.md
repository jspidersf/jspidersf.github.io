### Resort fit

**Protur Roquetas** has the clearest single-unit layout: its official Family Suite sleeps up to four adults plus two children in 61.3 m², with two bedrooms, a living room sofa bed and two bathrooms. Both bathrooms have baths, and the suite is on the fourth floor; confirm lift access. The hotel advertises a 2027 early-booking promotion, but no exact date/party price appeared. Its all-inclusive resort structure could help parental ease, while eating outside the resort and the packaged-resort atmosphere may be less attractive. The 22 October researcher's form request was malformed, so availability is unresolved.

**Hipotels Gran Conil** is a strong family/age fit: its official information lists a children's pool, indoor pool, family activities and Hipiclub groups for ages 3–7 and 8–12. The official room page describes the family room with one bedroom, separate sitting area and two single sofa beds, but accessible text did not state occupancy. No exact six-person room allocation or total was retrieved; likely two rooms, subject to live occupancy rules. It offers a sea-and-town setting, though the coastal access and summer town movement are less effortless than a flat beachfront resort.

**Fuerte Conil-Resort** has a compelling beach-and-town position: beachfront La Fontanilla, stated 10-minute walk to Conil old town, multiple children's pools, an indoor pool and Forti Club. Its official two-bedroom family room is explicitly limited to four, so the six-person party requires at least two rooms. Fuerte says room assignment can be between either of its buildings and cannot be guaranteed. No exact 2027 availability, two-room total or cancellation schedule was exposed.

### New western apartment lead

**Apartamento Ilusión del Mar con piscina**, Conil, is a stronger one-unit fit for space and amenities than the hotel family-room evidence: 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, six-person capacity, air conditioning in all rooms, rooftop shared pool, garage, and a claimed five-minute walk / 500 m to the sea. It sleeps six in one double plus four singles. A public August 2027 calendar displays €100/night, but only as a nightly tariff; it does not provide a selected six-person final total or unambiguous date availability. A 7-night €700 multiplication is merely arithmetic before unknown fees, not a quote. The listing says the apartment is on the second floor; elevator and stair count were not found. That is the main fit gap for the grandmothers. Cancellation terms are also unknown.

The apartment is a strong candidate to review if Jeremy is willing to check its booking/contact route, but it should remain a lead until accessibility, six-person availability and a final price are established. No contact or form submission was made.
