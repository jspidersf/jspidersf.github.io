## Apartment cancellation conflict

For a 29 July arrival, the selected property page advertises free cancellation through **28 June 2027**. La Vega's detailed selected-rate policy specifies 0% of rent until that date, 30% from 29 June to arrival, and 100% of the prepayment for no-show. For arrivals on 30 and 31 July, the headline free-cancellation dates are 29 and 30 June. No cutoff hour/time zone was established, and refund treatment of prepaid cleaning/utilities is unclear.

The **general contract linked from checkout conflicts with that policy**. It lists 10% at 30 days before arrival, 50% from 29 to seven days, and 100% less than seven days before arrival. It also provides for equal-or-better accommodation substitution for specified unforeseen circumstances. Do not rely on an unconditional refund promise: get the applicable schedule and treatment of every prepaid charge clarified in writing before booking.

Check-in is shown as 16:00–17:00 with office key collection. The notes describe out-of-hours code-box collection after full prepayment. Optional in-apartment check-in is €10 and was not selected.
